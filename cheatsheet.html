Tracks = kinds of sound (TYPE, SOUND's last page – per track and pattern)

A DRUMSa kit of 8 drums: KICK SNARE CLAP CH OH RIM SNAP PERC – pads 9–16 and the keys play them (GM notes over MIDI) · the closed hat chokes the open one · encoder B = the kit
B BASSthe 808: played like a bass over the keys – mono, PUNCH (pitch drop), CLICK, SUB, TONE, DRIVE soft / hard / fold, DECAY or GATE, SLIDE legato (overlapping notes glide) or always
C D MEL4-op FM: 8 algorithms, feedback on op 4, 8 voices, filter + envelopes, LFO, arp – bells, plucks, keys, pads
OBERVAVEthe analog poly: its 64 sounds and pages (OSC 1/2, VOICE, OSC MOD, FILTER)
REAL KITDRUMS, sounds 9–12: an acoustic kit (samples) – rows KICK · SNARE · TOM HI · HAT · OPEN · TOM LO · RIDE · CRASH; each drum's TUNE (64 = as recorded) and DECAY (127 = the whole hit), soft hits are duller · KIT page, knob 4 DRUMS: SYNTH / ACOUSTIC / HIP HOP switches any kit · HIP HOP (programs 13–16: NY KIT, BOOM BAP, NY DUSTY, NY HEAVY): KICK · SNARE · SNARE 2 · HAT · OPEN · RIM · KICK 2 · CRASH, the same drums tuned down, compressed and on an SP-1200 grid · the drums: Virtuosity Drums by Versilian Studios, CC0
PIANO ROLLSTEPS on a track that is no kit: 12 pitches x 16 steps. Knob 2 or a key picks the row (the pitch) · a pad sets that pitch on its step, a tap on a note takes that one note away (the step's other notes stay) · a key played writes its note at the cursor (added to what is there), the cursor goes on · EDIT clears the row's note at the cursor · ENTER: FOLD – only the pitches the track uses (and the chosen one) as rows, so the whole part is in view; again: all keys · marks at the edge: notes above / below the window
DRUM GRIDA drum played writes itself at the cursor (a key, MIDI, or SHIFT + K1–K6 = the kit's first six drums), the cursor goes on · a pad sets or removes the chosen row's drum on its step
MOUSEIn the plugin and the browser: click a cell in STEPS – that note or drum on that step goes on or off and sounds
DXa DX7 voice (6 operators, 32 algorithms) under four macros: BRIGHT · ATTACK · DECAY · RELEASE · TONE: ALGO, FEEDBACK (VOICE = as the sound has it) · FILTER · VOICE · slots 129–160 (encoder B): 20 factory sounds · F3 LOAD → DX BANK: a .syx bank from VIBE FM/boom/ puts its 32 sounds into those slots
STRINGSa string ensemble (8 voices), sounds 161–176: PERFORM ENSEMBLE · CUTOFF · ATTACK · RELEASE · REGISTERS: 16' · 8' · 4' · FILTER · VOICE – ENSEMBLE at 0 is the dry saws, full is the three swaying lines only
E-PIANOan electric piano (8 voices), sounds 193–200: MODEL TINE (the suitcase / stage sound) or REED (the reed piano) · PERFORM TONE · BARK · TREMOLO · DECAY · PIANO: MODEL · TONE · BELL/BITE · HAMMER · PICKUP: BARK · PREAMP · TOUCH · VOLUME · TREMOLO: DEPTH · RATE · STEREO (MONO = the amp's, L-R = the suitcase's) · FILTER · VOICE · AMP ENV: ATTACK · DECAY · DAMPER – play it hard and it barks, let it ring and it gets round
SAMPLERsounds 177–184, as ZVAMPLER · EDIT: the sample big, START green, END red (turning one zooms to it) · knobs START END PITCH FINE · F1 LOAD a WAV onto the track · F2 the input REC takes (IN 1 / IN 2 / RESAMPLE) · F3 1-SHOT / GATE / LOOP · F4 FWD / REV · F5 KEYS: MEL (the sample in tune, C3 = as recorded) or CHOP (C3 = slice 1) · REC on the track: records from the next bar until REC again · CHOP: the slices in order – keys from C3, pads row 3 = slices 1–4, row 4 = 5–8, SHIFT + K1–K6 = 1–6 (no scale, no octave) · knob 3 SLICES: how many · play a slice or knob 1: the cut · knob 2 MOVES it (SHIFT fine; cut 1 = START) · F1 ADD CUT · F2 DEL CUT · F3 EVEN again · F4 HITS: the cuts onto the hits · SOUND: QUALITY (CLEAN, SP 12, MPC, LO-FI), ATTACK, RELEASE, VOLUME
AUDIOany track, as in OberVave: IN 1 / IN 2 / RESAMPLE, takes per pattern, CUT, WAVs

Colour keys = sound & sequencer

SOUNDDRUMS: PERFORM · KICK · SNARE · CLAP · HATS · RIM/SNAP · PERC · KIT · TYPE  ·  BASS: PERFORM · BODY · PUNCH · GLIDE · DRIVE · FILTER · TYPE  ·  MEL: PERFORM · ALGO · OP 1–4 · FILTER · VOICE · TYPE · an audio track: AUDIO · TAKE · FILTER · TYPE · in STEPS: clear the step
ENVDRUMS: the 8 drums' LEVELS (2 pages) · BASS / MEL: filter envelope · amp envelope
LFOShape, rate, delay, phase · destinations pitch / filter / amp (BASS: wobble, MEL: vibrato)
ARPMode · division · octaves · gate (again: HOLD)
FXChorus · Delay (MODE ANALOG · DIGITAL · TAPE, time, feedback, tone) · Reverb (MODE HALL · ROOM · PLATE, size, damp, pre-delay) · Drive (TYPE OVERDRV · DIST · FUZZ · CRUSH, or AMP: a tube amp as an insert – turning it on puts the chosen track through its own amp, knob 4 (or the mixer's DRIVE row) sets how much of a track; GAIN, TONE) (again: next one) – delay and reverb return full: the mixer's sends set how much
SEQAgain and again: TRACKS → STEPS → TRACK → PATTERNS · STEPS: a strip on top shows the whole track (the bar being edited framed, the cursor, the playhead) · DRUMS in STEPS: the drum grid – a row a drum; encoder 2 (or a key) picks the row, the 16 pads are that drum's steps, encoder 3 its velocity, encoder 4 its ROLL (2 3 4 6 8 hits), SOUND clears that drum on the step · an audio track: STEPS = CUT

Encoders 1–4 = the four values shown above F1–F6. On a drum page, SHIFT held: the knobs of that page's drums are their LEVELS (KICK page: knob 4 = kick level; PERFORM: kick · snare · clap · hat).

TIME (notes, no bars): TRACK view, LENGTH below 1 = TIME (further down: OFF). REC starts the track at once and records freely, REC again (or STOP) closes the loop right there · the TRACK view shows the whole take as a time line with the tempo's beats and bars (1.2, 1.3 …) · knob 2 START, knob 3 END in seconds (slow ~1 ms, faster 8 / 32, fast turn a step) – they snap onto the beats they pass (SHIFT: free), the info line says END ON 1.4 · on a beat it holds a moment · SHIFT + REC: a new take · it follows the tempo.

TRACK view = a time line for every track: the notes where they play, how high and how long (brighter the harder), the beats and bars behind them · a DRUMS track: a lane a drum in its colour · an audio track: the wave with the same beats and bars, outside START – END dimmed, START green, END red.

SAMPLER, EDIT page: the input's level under the wave · SHIFT + 1 IN GAIN · SHIFT + 2 THRESHOLD (after REC: START on the first hit) · SHIFT + 3 REVERSE · F4 TRIM (twice): cuts away what lies outside START–END for good · F5 NORMALIZE (twice): the part START–END up to −0.3 dB, the whole sample gets that gain (it says how many dB first) · KEYS MEL / CHOP: on the CHOP page's F5 · REC records at once (stopped: from bar 1). A new sample stays in memory (NOT SAVED, red) until SAVE ▸ AUDIO gives it a name – no numbered copies on the card (GLOBAL, SHIFT + 3: AUTO SAVE on if you want them). The sample's name shows top left.

MIDI tracks (0.30 – an external synth: only out, nothing sounds in BOOM)

TYPESOUND ▸ TYPE: MIDI (slots 185–192: MIDI CH 1–8) · the steps, the keys, MIDI IN, the ARP and REC work as on any track – the notes go out
MIDICHANNEL 1–16 · PROGRAM NONE / 1–128 (sent once the knob rests, and when the track becomes MIDI) · VELOCITY AS PLAYED / fixed
PERFORMfour knobs = four CCs (sent when they change: a knob, automation, a sound loaded) · CC NUMBERS: which CC each one is (default 74 · 71 · 73 · 1)
OUTto every USB-MIDI device at the VIBE and to the computer · CLOCK and START/STOP go out while a MIDI track exists (GLOBAL MIDI OUT can stay off) · a note's OFF goes to the channel it started on

Audio tracks (on the VIBE: the inputs IN 1 / IN 2 – with an interface like the XR18: IN 1 … IN 16)

TYPESOUND, last page: SYNTH / AUDIO – per pattern: each pattern its own audio tracks and takes, they change with it on the bar
AUDIOSOURCE IN 1 … IN 16 (as many as the interface has; stereo: the pairs IN 1+2, 3+4 …) · RESAMPLE (what you hear) · CHANNELS mono / stereo · GAIN +0…40 dB (IN 1 / IN 2 only) (the input bar top right: red = clipping) · MONITOR
TAKEBARS (FREE: as long as recorded, rounded to bars · TIME, below 1 / below FREE: no bars at all – REC records at once, REC again ends it, the loop is exactly the take, START / END in seconds) · LOOP / ONCE (once from the pattern's start) · REC NEW / DUB (overdub) · INPUT line / mic / hi-z
FILTEROFF / LP / HP / BP · CUTOFF · RESO · DRIVE · in the sequencer: CUTOFF
ARMHold REC + push knob 1–4 (tracks A–D, or E–H in that window) · hold REC + ENTER: the chosen track · hold REC + a pad of row 1 – that audio track ARMED or not (any page, several at once) · armed: its letter white on red in the header, ARM · IN n and the input's level in TRACKS, a red frame in its view · REC then records all armed tracks at once, each its own take, from the same frame – multitrack
RECStopped: armed, PLAY records from bar 1 · playing: from the next bar · REC again: stop (all) · held and let go: clear the take (not after arming) · nothing armed: the chosen audio track
CUTFROM · TO (a detent = 1/16, fast = a bar, SHIFT = 1 ms) · ERASE / DELETE / CROP · DO: turn twice
START ENDAn audio track's TRACK view (BARS · MODE · START · END): the loop plays only START to END of the take (bar.beat.sixteenth+ms, END exclusive), free: turned slowly each detent goes to the next rising zero crossing (no click at the seam), faster in bigger steps, SHIFT: a frame – nothing is cut · in TIME the loop is START to END in seconds, it runs free of the bars – START / END snap onto the beats they pass (SHIFT: free)

FILES (F3 LOAD · F4 SAVE, encoder 1 the kind, ENTER does it; all in VIBE FM/boom/ on the card)

PRESET one sound (.obp) – SAVE: the track's sound with a name (encoder 2 place, 3 letter) · LOAD: into the track, edited (F5 STORE keeps it in a slot) · PATTERN (.obt) the pattern with its sounds, mixer and audio tracks, the takes as WAVs beside it – LOAD (ENTER twice; encoder 3: which pattern, the one you are on only stopped) replaces that pattern, its audio follows · AUDIO – SAVE: the track's take as a WAV · LOAD: IN MEMORY a take another pattern plays (the same take in several patterns) or any WAV from card or USB stick (16/24 bit, float; other rates converted) – encoder 3 / 4: into which pattern and track · SONG (ALL) (.obv) all 64 patterns, sounds, mixers, settings and audio – LOAD replaces everything (ENTER twice) · NEW SONG (first in the list, ENTER twice) empties every pattern and its audio; settings and programs stay

In the DAW (VIBE FM VST 0.9)

FOCUS · one knob, one button

Hold ENTER (or the DATA wheel) 0.6 s: an orange frame walks over the area, track, sound, the four values and F1–F6 with DATA · ENTER uses it (a value: DATA changes, ENTER ends) · hold again or ↩: off. A short ENTER stays BOOM's.
